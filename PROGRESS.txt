What this shows:
- With a failing button, the agent usually still SAYS it succeeded: the
  pixel rule accepts small unrelated changes.
- Slow: any small change is accepted at once, so the agent does not wait.
  Success is judged when the agent finishes; a delayed effect may appear
  later.
- Pop-up: it gave up honestly. The planner cannot close pop-ups.
- Normal pages: only 1 task differed between pixel rule and no checking
  (to-do recovery). Far too small to conclude the rule helps.
- To-do: "task" matched the heading "My tasks"; recovery guessed "below
  the label", which happened to hit the box (luck of this layout).

WHY 10/10 IS OPTIMISTIC:
- The same AI wrote the sites, instructions, planner and matching rules,
  and had seen the validation pages. The planner only knows these
  patterns; its word lists are from the instructions plus general
  synonyms, but this is not independent.
- Pages are simple; many labels are clickable HTML labels, which helps
  text-only matching. Real sites may not behave like this.
- One run per task, one window size.
